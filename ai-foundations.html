ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Build AI literacy with curiosity and care.

What students can explore

Students need more than exposure to new tools. They need ways to ask questions, evaluate outputs, and understand the responsibilities that come with using AI.

  • What AI is and where it appears in everyday life
  • How data, models, and patterns relate to AI
  • Guided creative and problem-solving activities
  • Accuracy, bias, privacy, and responsible use
  • Human judgment and critical evaluation of outputs
