BUSINESS & ENTREPRENEURSHIP

From a first idea to a thoughtful pitch.

What students can explore

Entrepreneurship activities give students a structured way to identify needs, test ideas, and communicate value.

  • Spotting problems and opportunities in a community
  • Developing an idea and value proposition
  • Understanding customers, costs, and simple budgets
  • Building a brand or prototype concept
  • Collaborating and presenting a pitch
01

Discover

Notice a need, ask questions, and understand who a solution could help.

02

Create

Work together to develop an idea, simple plan, and project concept.

03

Present

Explain the idea, listen to feedback, and reflect on what could improve.
