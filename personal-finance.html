PERSONAL FINANCE

From money concepts to everyday confidence.

What students can explore

Financial learning is most useful when students can connect it to choices they may face. Activities can be adapted to student age and school priorities.

  • Needs, wants, and spending priorities
  • Budgeting, saving, and setting goals
  • How banking, credit, interest, and borrowing work
  • Making trade-offs in realistic scenarios
  • Planning for short- and long-term goals
01

Build vocabulary and understand core financial concepts in an accessible way.

Practice
